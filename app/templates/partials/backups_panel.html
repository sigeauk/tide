How to restore a backup

On the host, in TIDE's folder. A backup can go back into the TIDE version that made it, the version at the end of its name, or into a newer one, which upgrades its databases as it starts; never into an older one. Your current databases are moved to a data-before-restore-<date> folder, not deleted.
